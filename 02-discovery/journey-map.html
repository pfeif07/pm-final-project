A fair way forward

Future-state journey: help people in a debt collection scenario choose and agree to an affordable, sustainable instalment plan in self service, with no staff interaction.

Value propositionUnderstand your case, choose a plan for multiple claims. Do it on your phone in minutes.

PersonaShift-working consumer with two or three open claims and tight finances, on a phone during breaks.
GoalSettle all claims with one affordable plan they understand, without more cost or pressure.
Friction todayUnclear income question, one claim at a time, fear of misuse, then a fall back to the agent.
  1. 1

    See all claims

    Minute 1

    User action

    Open the link, see every claim together → know what's owed before choosing.

    Internal state

    Claims linked, progress auto-saved → resume after a break without restarting.

    Pain point addressed

    Status shown per claim → clear whether paying one affects the other's letters.

    N-03, T-202
  2. 2

    Share income

    Minutes 2 to 3

    User action

    Answer income in plain-language options → no guessing this month versus a normal month.

    Internal state

    Answers saved at every step → back button and interruptions lose nothing.

    Pain point addressed

    Explain why income is asked and how long kept → eases "what if they use it against me."

    Declare income, no document upload → no dead end waiting for a file.

    N-01, N-06, T-206, T-201, T-204
  3. 3

    Pick a plan

    Minute 4

    User action

    Compare plans covering all claims → choose the one that fits the budget.

    Internal state

    Plans calculated from saved answers → only sustainable options are offered.

    Pain point addressed

    Every payment date and amount shown → no surprise second payment after picking fewest months.

    N-02
  4. 4

    Agree & track

    Minute 5

    User action

    Review the summary and confirm → know exactly what's agreed, with no staff call.

    Internal state

    Plan agreed, schedule set, reminders queued → journey logged as completed, not just started.

    Pain point addressed

    Reminders sent before the due date → no late surprises or extra cost.

    T-207

Three advantages over the manual workaround

  1. One plan across all claimsAgent route typically resolves one claim at a time.
  2. Self service on a phone, on breaksNo call windows, hold times or call-backs for shift workers.
  3. Consistent explanations, saved answers, timely remindersLess pressure, fewer drop-offs, and completions you can measure.
Evidence: T-201 (853 journeys waiting on a document that never arrived), T-204 (back button wipes affordability answers), T-207 (reminders sometimes arrive after the due date).
