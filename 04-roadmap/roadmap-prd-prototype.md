## Roadmap
### NOW, this sprint
- **B1 One plan for several open claims**, This is the moment of misery itself. Without it, the +10% multi-claim conversion goal has no mechanism.
- **B3 Affordability check before the plan options**, B1 is only useful if the plan is affordable. It also prevents missed second payments (N-02), which protects the call-in guardrail.

### NEXT, the following sprints
- **B2 Plain explainer for why income is asked and how it is used**, It removes the “what if they use it against me” drop-off (N-06, T-206) and reduces calls. Copy and design only.
- **B5 Photo upload that works on mobile, or skip the document under a set amount**, T-201 shows 853 journeys stalled on a document that never arrived. Split it, as noted below.

### LATER, backlog
- **B4 Handoff summary for the agent when a consumer calls**, It helps agents once she calls, but does nothing for conversion or call-in rate. This is an Ops preference.
- **B6 Pick a payment date that matches pay day	**, It helps variable shift income stay on plan, but it follows B1 and B3 and isn’t the core friction.

### ✂ Cut List
- **B7 Show the shortest plan first to raise recovery	**, It steers her to the plan she is most likely to miss (N-02), which pushes calls up. It serves recovery targets, not the persona.
- **B8 Chat assistant on every portal page**, It is large and compliance-heavy in a collections context. It risks becoming a second route to the agent, and it doesn’t fix the multi-claim flow.
