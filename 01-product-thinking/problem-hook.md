The core tension

Riverty can show consumers a consistent journey to understand a case, pay, or dispute. For the consumer who cannot pay in one go, the journey fragments across the portal, letters, decision logic and agent processes. Those are the people with the highest need and the highest cost to serve, and they are where self-service breaks.

What happens over 12 months
Months 0-3 - Consumers dont select a payment strategy and therefore dont pay the open claims. - Success rates for clients stay low while costs for claim recovery increase
Months 3-6 - The more we get in touch with the consumer, the more frustrated the person will be and may agree to something but not what they would like to do - Satisfaction decreases, consumers miss payments, consumers call in which increases costs even more
Months 6-9 - Success rate stays low, due to increased costs, clients have to pay more - clients become unsatisfied; new rates make it more difficult for sales to sell the service
Months 9-12	- Clients may be unsatisfied and select a different provider
